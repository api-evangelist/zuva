The National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) enforces labor laws in the United States, specifically those related to collective bargaining and unfair labor practices. On February 21, 2023, the NLRB issued a ruling in McLaren Macomb , 372 NLRB No. 58 (2023) about, among other things, a Severance Agreement that contained confidentiality and non-disparagement provisions.
