Zuva releases Multi-Level Document Classifier via its API Offering Zuva and Litera are partnering with the Standards Advancement for the Legal Industry (SALI) Alliance to release an open-source document classification taxonomy. This project to build an enhanced multi-level document classifier is the culmination of nearly a decade of work started at Kira in 2014, evolving into a collaboration as Kira was acquired by Litera in 2021 and Zuva spun off into a separate corporation.
